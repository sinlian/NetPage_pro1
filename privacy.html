Studio Terms

Privacy

What we store, why, and how to make it disappear.

What we store

Your account (email and sign-in method), the photos you upload, generated renders, and the credit ledger needed to bill renders correctly. Nothing else.

How photos are used

Uploads are used only to generate your clip and are passed to the video model for that purpose. They are never sold, shared for advertising, or used to train models.

Retention

Uploads and renders are kept for up to 30 days, then purged automatically. Download anything you want to keep before then.

Your deletion controls

From the account page you can delete your uploads, your renders, or your entire account at any time. Deletion is immediate and irreversible.

Local storage

Your browser keeps a session token and your language choice locally. Before you sign in, selected photos stay on your device only.
