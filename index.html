What exactly is DuoStage?

It is the two-photo duet format: two subjects trading verses around a suspended microphone inside a saturated orange room. DuoStage is an independent generator for that format 鈥?your portraits provide the performers while a fixed reference clip provides the scene, the motion and the camera timing. There is no timeline to edit and no prompt to write.
