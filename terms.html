Terms

The rules for credits, content and availability.

Credits

Packs are one-time purchases: no subscription, no auto-renewal. Credits never expire and stacks add up. A generation reserves its credits while pending.

Success, failure, refunds

A successful render consumes its credits even if you re-render. A confirmed failure returns them. Credit returns are separate from payment refunds.

Your content rights

You warrant you have the right to use every portrait you upload, including consent from the people shown. Illegal, harmful or non-consensual content is prohibited.

Moderation

Uploads and outputs pass moderation. A rejection fails the job and returns its credits; repeated abuse can end account access.

Minors

The service is for users 18 and older, or minors with verifiable guardian consent.

Availability

Renders are hosted for a limited window (30 days) and then purged. Download copies of anything you want to keep; re-downloads are free while hosted.
